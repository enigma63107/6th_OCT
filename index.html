Groq key

The app uses Groq's free speech AI to understand English, Hindi and Gujarati. Get a free key at console.groq.com/keys and paste it here. It's stored only on this phone.

How it works

  1. Tap Listen to them. You'll see and hear what the other person says in Gujarati.
  2. Hold the green button and reply in Hindi or English. The app shows and says the Gujarati.
  3. Hold it again and say that sentence in Gujarati. You'll get a score.

Earphones help: the other person won't hear the app, and it won't hear itself.
